## Lessons / failures
- 2026-11-14 12:10 · Jusef (DATA) · Profiling before mapping overturned three assumptions in the brief: taxis are an MCC inside Transportation (not a category), there are no subscriptions or recurring merchants, and rent never really rises (±10%). `transaction_type` values are snake_case (`housing_payment`), and 5% of purchases have no merchant or MCC
- 2026-11-14 12:55 · Jusef (COACH) · Gemini API latency varies a lot during the event (single calls 1.4–13.5 s; one 504 at our 15 s deadline). Gemma 4 through Google's API is much slower (answers 12.6–50.9 s; 504s at a 15 s per-call limit). Fix: per-provider timeouts and a fallback model. The privacy-toggle demo should not depend on stage-1 Gemma being fast
